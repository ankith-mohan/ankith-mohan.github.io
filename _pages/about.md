I am a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the [Manning College of Information and Computer Sciences](https://www.cics.umass.edu) at the [University of Massachusetts Amherst](https://www.umass.edu), where I work with [Gayane Vardoyan](https://people.cs.umass.edu/~gvardoyan) and [Philip S. Thomas](https://people.cs.umass.edu/~pthomas/). I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from [Virginia Tech](https://vt.edu), where I was advised by [Jamie Sikora](https://sites.google.com/site/jamiesikora), and my M.S. in Computer Science from the [University of Southern California](https://usc.edu), where I worked with [Emilio Ferrara](https://viterbi.usc.edu/directory/faculty/Ferrara/Emilio) and [Aiichiro Nakano](https://viterbi.usc.edu/directory/faculty/Nakano/Aiichiro).

My current research focuses on **quantum information** and **quantum networks**. I am particularly interested in developing algorithms and protocols for quantum communication and distributed quantum systems, with an emphasis on quantum repeater networks, entanglement distribution, and the use of optimization theory and reinforcement learning for network control.
