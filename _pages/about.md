I am a PhD Candidate at [Virginia Tech](https://vt.edu) advised by [Jamie Sikora](https://website.cs.vt.edu/people/faculty/jamie-sikora.html). I received my M.S. from the [University of Southern California](https://usc.edu), where I was fortunate to be advised by [Emilio Ferrara](https://viterbi.usc.edu/directory/faculty/Ferrara/Emilio) and [Aiichiro Nakano](https://viterbi.usc.edu/directory/faculty/Nakano/Aiichiro).

My research lies at the intersection of **quantum computing**, **machine learning**, and **optimization**.
